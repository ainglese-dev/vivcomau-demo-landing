# VIVCOM Landing Page

Single-page marketing site for VIVCOM, a Sydney/NSW low-voltage & telecom install company merging its services with VIV53 LLC's remote IT consulting/automation practice. Customers see one brand (VIVCOM); the two-tier service split and lead routing are internal.

## Language

**Tier 1 (Local Installs)**:
Physical, NSW-based install services delivered on-site — CCTV, AV, Telecom/Cabling, and Smart Hands. Primary Google Ads landing target; appears first on the page.
_Avoid_: Residential tier, install tier

**Tier 2 (Consulting & Automation)**:
Remote B2B services delivered by VIV53 — DC Automation, Compliance/AI, Cloud/DevOps. Secondary ad group; appears after Tier 1.
_Avoid_: Enterprise tier, VIV53 tier

**Smart Hands**:
A Tier 1 service: on-site technical labor at NSW datacenters performed on behalf of remote companies without local staff. Full remote-hands scope — cabling, hardware swaps, power cycling, visual inspections, rack-and-stack.
_Avoid_: Remote hands (industry-interchangeable, but "Smart Hands" is VIVCOM's chosen term)

**Lead Routing**:
The rule for who gets notified of a contact-form submission. Resolved as flat, not tier-based: every submission notifies the same fixed recipient list (Juan + the VIV53 principal), regardless of which service tier was selected. There is no branching on the service field.
_Avoid_: Lead assignment, ticket routing
